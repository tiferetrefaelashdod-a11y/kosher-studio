Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Kosher Studio ("the app") is an Android app for managing your own YouTube channel. This policy explains what data the app accesses and what it does with it.

Use of YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What the app accesses

When you connect a channel, you approve access through Google's own sign-in page. With that approval the app can:

  • read your channel's name and picture, to show which channel is connected;
  • upload videos and thumbnails that you choose on your phone;
  • read and update the title, description, tags, category and visibility of your videos;
  • create, read, update and delete your scheduled live events.

The app only does these things when you ask it to.

Where your data is stored

  • The app has no servers. All communication is directly between your phone and Google/YouTube.
  • The sign-in token and the list of connected channels are stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage.
  • Videos and images you pick are read from your phone and sent only to YouTube.
  • The app does not collect analytics and does not include advertising or tracking code.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone. Data is sent only to Google/YouTube to perform the actions you request.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data and revoking access

  • Remove a channel inside the app (Accounts → Remove), or uninstall the app, to delete everything it stored on the phone.
  • You can revoke the app's access at any time on the Google security settings page: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
